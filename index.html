Think. Walk. Thalk about it.

Some of your clearest thinking happens on a walk. Science agrees — walking increases creative thinking by up to 81%. Thalk gives those moments a place to live, so you can find them again years from now.

How it works

Step 01

Go for a walk

Start a Thalk and let GPS track your route while you think.

Step 02

Capture the moment

Add photos, the song you’re listening to, and a voice memo or written thoughts.

Step 03

Revisit your past self

Browse by year and month. A random memory surfaces daily — where you walked, what you felt, who you were.

“Thalk” — a state of confusion; thinking too fast and being unable to express your thoughts in words.
