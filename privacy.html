Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Overview

Thalk is a personal walking journal app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the app handles your data.

Data storage

All data you create in Thalk — your name, walks, GPS routes, photos, voice memos, and written thoughts — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

Because your data is stored only on your device, if your device is lost, damaged, or reset and you do not have iCloud Device Backup enabled, your Thalks may be lost. We recommend keeping iCloud Backup on.

Location data

Thalk uses your device’s GPS to track your walking route during an active Thalk. This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or shared with third parties.

Photos

Thalk requests permission to access your Photo Library so you can attach existing photos to a Thalk, and permission to use the Camera so you can take new photos within the app. Photos remain on your device.

Microphone

Thalk requests permission to use the microphone to record voice memos during a Thalk. Voice memos are stored only on your device.

Spotify integration

If you choose to connect Spotify, Thalk uses the Spotify API solely to search for and tag songs you associate with a Thalk. We do not store your Spotify credentials on our servers, access your listening history, or share any data with Spotify beyond what is required for the search functionality.

Analytics and tracking

Thalk does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or user tracking services. We do not collect usage data about how you use the app.

App updates

Thalk uses Expo’s update service to deliver app updates. When the app launches, it contacts Expo’s servers to check whether a new version is available. No personal data from your Thalks is sent during this check.

Third parties

Aside from the Spotify search and Expo update check described above, we do not share or transmit your personal data to any third parties. We do not sell your data.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because Thalk stores your data only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly by editing or deleting Thalks within the app, or by deleting the app entirely.

For any additional requests, contact us at the email below and we will respond within 30 days.

Children’s privacy

Thalk is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or any data-related requests, contact us at: business.thalk@gmail.com
